How patients get into the app
  1. Register the patient here with their own mobile number.
  2. You get a 6-digit code. Tell or write it for the patient (valid 3 days).
  3. On their phone, the patient opens ArogyaSaathi → First time? Activate account, enters mobile + code and makes a password.
  4. Lost the code? Open the patient in Patients and tap New code.
